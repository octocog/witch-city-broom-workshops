Private groups and parties

Book the room and the session is yours alone. Same makers, same materials, your music and your people.

How a private session works

Seven or more makers closes the room to the public. You pick the date and start time, including evenings. We set the bench for your group, play what you want to hear, and leave time at the end for photos with the finished brooms. Then we ship every broom home, so nobody carries one to dinner.

Bring your own snacks and non alcoholic drinks. Two hours is enough: ninety minutes of making and a half hour of settling in and showing off.

  • Bachelorette and bachelor weekends
  • Birthdays and anniversaries
  • Team outings and offsites
  • School, scout and youth groups
  • Bridal and baby showers
  • Family reunions and holiday parties

Private sessions are $135 per maker with a minimum of seven. Up to twelve at the bench. Larger groups split into back to back sessions. Ask about school and nonprofit rates.
