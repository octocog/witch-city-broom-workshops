Privacy

What this website collects, what the booking system collects, and how to reach us about any of it.

What this site collects

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It also records which ad brought you here, if one did, so we know which ads are worth running. None of that identifies you by name.

What the booking system collects

When you book, you enter your name, email, phone number and payment details on FareHarbor, our booking partner. FareHarbor keeps that information to run your booking and send your confirmation. We see the booking details and use them only to run your session and to reach you about it. Payment card numbers never reach us. FareHarbor's own privacy policy is at fareharbor.com/privacy.

Forms

The contact and private group forms send what you type to our email through Formspree, which handles the delivery. We use it to answer you and for nothing else.

Email

We do not send a newsletter. If we ever start one, you will be asked first, and every message will have a way to stop.

Cookies

Analytics and advertising cookies are the only ones this site sets. Blocking them in your browser does not affect booking.

Your information
