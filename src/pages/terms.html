Terms

Plain terms for booking and attending a workshop.

Booking

A booking is confirmed when payment clears on FareHarbor and you receive a confirmation email. Each seat covers one maker and one broom. Prices are in US dollars and include all materials and tax where it applies.

Changes and cancellations

Covered in full on the cancellation policy page. In short: free up to 48 hours before, no refund inside 48 hours, send a friend in your place any time.

Ages

Makers are ten and up. Anyone under sixteen needs a booked adult making alongside them.

At the bench

The tools include a large needle, cord under tension and a blade for trimming. Makers follow the instructions given in the room. We may stop a session for anyone whose behavior puts others at risk, without a refund.

Your broom

You keep what you make. If you leave it with us to ship, we pack it and send it to the address you give at the bench, and email you the tracking number. Check the address carefully, since we ship to what you write down. We may photograph sessions for our own website and social media. Tell a maker at check in if you would rather not appear, and we will keep you out of the frame.

Gift cards

Gift cards are sold through FareHarbor, do not expire, are not refundable, and can be redeemed by anyone holding the code toward any booking on this site.

Changes to these terms

We will update this page when something changes and note the date here. Last updated November 2026.
