Gift cards

Give a seat at the bench. The card arrives by email the same minute and works on any open date.

How it works

Pick an amount, add a note, and send it to them or to yourself. One seat is $135. A table for four is $540. The card is a code they enter at checkout when they book a date that suits them.

Cards do not expire. If the price changes before they redeem it, the card covers what it was worth when you bought it and they pay any difference.

Gift card questions

Can they pick their own date?

Yes. The card comes as a code. They book whatever open session they like and enter the code at checkout.

Can I buy for more than one person?

Yes. Buy any amount. A card worth $270 covers two seats, $540 covers four, and so on. Or buy one card per person if you want each to have their own.

What if the session they want is full?

They pick another. Sessions run most days year round, and the card has no expiry, so there is no rush.

Can I print it?

The email includes a printable card. Many people tuck it inside a Witch City Broom Co. whisk from the shop on Essex Street.
