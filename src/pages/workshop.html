The broom making workshop

Ninety minutes at the bench, one full size witch's broom, and the thing you made riding home with you.

What you make

A besom, the round witch's broom of folklore, and this is the real deal, not a toy kit. A natural wood handle, a full head of broomcorn gathered around it and bound tight, and dried flowers and greenery tucked into the binding. It stands about four feet tall.

Then you make it yours. Choose the broomcorn colors, the ribbons, and the charms and trinkets that hang from the binding. Each color and charm carries a meaning, from protection to good luck, and your maker walks you through them. Most people hang theirs by the door. Some keep it by the altar.

Everything is included in the $135: the handle, the broomcorn, the dried flowers and greenery, the ribbons, the charms, and a maker at your elbow the whole time. When it is done, we can ship it home for you.

How the ninety minutes go

No experience needed. Every tool is on the bench when you arrive. Our makers work the room so nobody falls behind.

  1. 0:00

    Pick your handle

    Every handle is natural wood with its own bend and grain. Choose yours, then the broomcorn colors to go with it.

  2. 0:15

    Gather and bind

    Arrange the broomcorn around the handle and bind it tight. A maker helps you pull it secure. This is the part people remember.

  3. 0:40

    Add your earthly elements

    Tuck dried flowers and greenery into the binding. Your maker explains what each one is said to bring.

  4. 1:05

    Ribbons and charms

    Wrap the binding in ribbon and tie on charms and trinkets for protection, for luck, or just because you like them.

  5. 1:30

    Fly home

    Carry it out the door, or leave it with us and we ship it to your house. No lugging it around Salem, no squeezing it onto a plane.

Who it suits

Anyone who wants to leave Salem holding something they made. Couples, friends on a weekend, families with kids of any age, people who have never made a thing and people who craft every weekend.

It is indoors, it is seated for most of the ninety minutes, and it does not need strong hands. The binding takes a firm pull, and a maker will help with that part if you want it.

Twelve makers at most per session. If you are more than six, a private session gives you the room to yourselves.

What to bring

Nothing. Wear something you do not mind getting a little dusty, since broomcorn sheds. Leave the big bags at the hotel, the benches are close together.

Getting it home

Leave it with us. We pack your broom and ship it to your door, so you are not carrying a four foot broom through the rest of your trip or working out how to get it on a plane. Give us the address at the bench and we handle everything from there: careful packing, the trip to the shipping company, and the tracking number in your inbox. Flat rate shipping is available and is paid separately from the workshop price. If you would rather walk out with it, that is fine too. Most people who do get stopped for photos on Essex Street.
