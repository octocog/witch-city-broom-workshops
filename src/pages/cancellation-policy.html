Cancellation policy

Plans change. Here is what happens when they do.

Up to 48 hours before your session

Change the date, change the number of seats, or cancel for a full refund. Use the link in your confirmation email and it happens instantly. No call needed.

Inside 48 hours

The seat is yours and the materials are cut for you, so we cannot refund it. You can send someone else in your place at no charge. Tell us their name by email or phone before the session starts.

If we cancel

On the rare day we have to cancel, for weather, illness or a building problem, you get a full refund or a seat on another date, your choice, and an email the moment we know.

Late arrivals

Everyone winds at the same time, so we cannot restart the first steps for late makers. If you are running more than fifteen minutes behind, call {{PHONE}} and we will move you to the next open session if there is one that day.

Gift cards

Gift cards are not refundable, do not expire, and can be used by anyone who has the code.
