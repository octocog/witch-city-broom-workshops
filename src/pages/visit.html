Parking

Museum Place garage on Church Street is a four minute walk and the easiest bet. The South Harbor garage on Congress Street is a backup. Street parking downtown is metered and scarce in October.

By train

The Newburyport/Rockport commuter rail from Boston North Station stops at Salem in about 30 minutes. The workshop is a ten minute walk from the station, straight down Washington Street.

By ferry

In season the Salem Ferry runs from Long Wharf in Boston to Blaney Street. From the dock it is a fifteen minute walk along Derby Street.

When you arrive

Come ten minutes early. Check in at the front bench, pick your handle, and find your seat. The session starts on the hour and we do not hold the first steps for late arrivals, since everyone winds together.

Wear something you do not mind getting dusty. Tie back long hair. Leave big bags at the hotel if you can.

Getting around the room

The workshop is on one level with a step free entrance from Derby Square. Benches are 30 inches high with movable stools, and we can set a lower work surface or a wheelchair space with a day's notice. The restroom is on the same floor.
