Questions and answers

Everything we get asked on the phone, written down once.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Most makers have never touched broomcorn. A maker demonstrates each step at the front and then walks the room while you do it. If a cord slips, someone is there.

How long is the workshop?

Ninety minutes. Arrive ten minutes before your start time so the first handle goes on at the hour.

How much does it cost?

$135 per person. That covers the handle, the broomcorn, cord, trim, charms, ribbons and the finished broom you take home. There is nothing extra to buy.

Can kids take part?

Yes. All ages are welcome. The winding takes a firm pull and the stitching uses a large needle, so a maker helps younger children with those parts.

Can I bring someone who is not making a broom?

Yes. You are welcome to bring a guest, free of charge. They can sit with you, take photos and cheer you on. Only the people making a broom need a seat booked.

How many people are in a session?

Twelve at most. Groups of seven or more can book the room privately so the session is theirs alone.

Where is the workshop?

32 Derby Square, Salem, Massachusetts, behind Old Town Hall and two blocks from the Witch City Broom Co. shop on Essex Street. Museum Place garage is the closest parking.

What should I wear?

Anything you do not mind getting dusty. Broomcorn sheds a little. Tie back long hair for the winding. Closed shoes are sensible but not required.

How do I get the broom home?

Leave it with us and we ship it to your door. Give us the address at the end of the session and we pack it carefully, take it to the shipping company and email you the tracking. Flat rate shipping is available, paid separately from the workshop price. We take care of the rest. It is the favorite part for a lot of guests, since nobody wants to carry a four foot broom through the rest of a Salem weekend or fly home with it. If you would rather take it with you, you can. It is light and about four feet long.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 48 hours before your session, using the link in your confirmation email. Inside 48 hours the seat is yours and we cannot refund it, but you can send someone else in your place.

Is the workshop accessible?

The room is on one level with a step free entrance, and we can set a lower work surface or a wheelchair space with a day's notice. Tell us what would help when you book. More here.

Do you run sessions in October?

Yes, more than any other month, and they sell out weeks ahead. If you are coming for Haunted Happenings, book the workshop first and plan the rest of the day around it.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes. Gift cards are emailed on purchase, cover any amount, never expire, and work on any open date.

Is this the same as the Witch City Broom Co. shop?

Same family, different door. The shop at 246 Essex Street sells finished brooms and take home kits. The workshop at 32 Derby Square is where you make one yourself.
