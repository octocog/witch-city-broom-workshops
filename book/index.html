Before you book

Each seat makes one broom. Ages ten and up, with a booked adult beside anyone under sixteen. Arrive ten minutes early so the first handle goes on at the hour. When you are done, leave the broom with us and we ship it home.

Changing plans

Move or cancel free up to 48 hours before your session, straight from your confirmation email. Full policy.

More than six?

Groups of seven or more get the room to themselves. Ask about a private session and we will hold a date while you gather the group.
