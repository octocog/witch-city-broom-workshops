Accessibility

The workshop is for everyone who wants to make a broom. Here is how the room and this website are set up, and how to tell us when something gets in the way.

The workshop

  • The room is on one level with a step free entrance from Derby Square. The restroom is on the same floor.
  • Benches are 30 inches high with movable stools. With a day's notice we set a lower work surface or a clear wheelchair space at the bench.
  • Each step is shown at the front and then explained at your seat. If you would like the demonstration repeated up close, ask. Makers will also do any step for you, including the winding, which takes a firm pull.
  • Written step cards are on every bench. Large print versions are available on request.
  • Service animals are welcome. Broomcorn sheds, so we keep the floor swept during sessions.
  • Sessions can be loud when twelve people are winding at once. If a quieter session would help, the first session on weekday mornings is usually the smallest.

This website

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone and with screen readers. There is a skip link at the top of each page, headings follow a logical order, and every image has a text description.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200 percent without anything breaking, and colors were chosen for strong contrast.
  • Animations are kept to a minimum and switch off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Booking runs on FareHarbor, a separate service. If the calendar gives you trouble, call or email and we will book the seat for you.

Tell us

If something on the site or in the room does not work for you, we want to know. Call (978) 815-0013 or email hello@witchcitybroomworkshops.com and we will fix what we can and find another way for the rest.
