About the workshop

A bench in downtown Salem where visitors make the broom they came to buy.

Why we teach it

People love the handmade brooms at our sister company, Witch City Broom Co. but they wanted to know HOW we make them. People wanted to add the broom corn and pull the cord tight themselves.

So we opened a workshop at 32 Derby Square and put the bench on the guest's side. Ninety minutes later they walk out with a broom nobody else could have made the same exact way.

The workshops are part of the Witch City Experiences family, alongside Witch City Walking Tours and the Broom Co. shop. Many visitors do all three in a day!

And nobody has to carry a broom while they do it. We ship your finished broom home for you.

The craft

A besom is the oldest kind of broom there is, a bundle gathered around a handle and bound tight. It is the round broom of folklore, the one that leans by the hearth in every old story, and it is the one you make here.

We build ours with broomcorn, a sorghum grown for its long seed heads, in natural and dyed colors. Handles are natural wood, each with its own bend and grain. Dried flowers, greenery, ribbons and charms finish it, and each one is chosen for what it is said to bring: protection, good luck, a fresh start.

Salem's tie to the broom is older than the tourist season. Brooms stood by every colonial hearth here long before the trials made the town famous. Yours will stand by a door somewhere far from here and say Salem every time someone walks past it.
